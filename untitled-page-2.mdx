## Creative Variants

**Planned release: 27 October 2026 — draft, not yet released.**

Upload one video and choose the resolution that fits your inventory, without preparing each version manually.

### What is changing

Video uploads will be processed asynchronously, so you can continue working while video variants are generated. The original file will be kept as the **Master**, alongside three standard variants:

Each uploaded file and generated variant will have its own unique Creative ID. You will be able to select and assign each variant independently to the applicable inventory or campaign workflow.

### How to use it after release

1. Open **Libraries → Media Library** and upload a video creative.
2. Continue working while the video is processed. Wait for processing to finish before selecting a generated variant.
3. Choose the Master, High, Medium, or Low version that fits your target inventory.
4. Assign the selected variant in your inventory or campaign workflow. Each variant can be assigned independently.

No customer action is required before release. Generated variants will be available only after processing completes successfully.

### What stays the same

Images and Raw HTML5 creatives will not be transcoded. This planned update covers Creative Variants only; tagging, compliance, analytics, and a broader interface redesign are not part of this release.

For the existing upload workflow, see [Upload Creative](/documentation/creative-management/media-library/upload-creative).

If a variant is missing or processing fails after release, contact your Lynx support team with the Master creative name, upload time, and expected resolution.
