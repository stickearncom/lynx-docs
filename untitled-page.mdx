---
title: "IAB Venue Type 2.0"
---

Classify your screens more consistently with standardized **IAB Venue Type** and **IAB Sub Venue Type**, based on the OpenOOH/IAB Venue Taxonomy v1.2.1.

This update makes inventory easier to describe and gives programmatic buyers more consistent venue context.

#### What's new

- **Two-level venue classification:** Select a Venue Type and its related Sub Venue Type, such as **Retail → Mall** or **Transit → Airport**.

- **Consistent screen information:** Venue classification applies to **Roadside, Place-Based, and Fleet** inventory—not only screens enabled for programmatic.
- **Programmatic venue signals:** Programmatic inventory uses the venue classification from its linked master screen.

#### Why this matters

- Standardizes inventory using the **OpenOOH/IAB Venue Taxonomy v1.2.1**.
- Helps programmatic DOOH buyers understand and target the physical environment around a screen.
- Improves venue-data consistency across media owners, SSPs, DSPs, and buyers.

#### What you need to do

When creating or editing a screen:

1. Select the screen specification: **Roadside**, **Place-Based**, or **Fleet**.
2. Choose the required **IAB Venue Type (See Venue Type ↗)**.
3. Choose the corresponding **IAB Sub Venue Type (See Sub Venue Type ↗)**.
4. For Place-Based inventory, select the applicable **Placement** when available.
5. Save your changes.

Choose the venue based on the screen's physical environment, not its resolution or advertising size.

<Note>
  Existing screen IDs remain unchanged, and screens do not need to be re-registered. This update does not affect how impressions are generated, counted, or reported. Venue and Sub-venue data support programmatic DOOH requirements and venue standardization; selecting a venue does not change impression measurement or reported totals.
</Note>
