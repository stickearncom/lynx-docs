PMP Deal Creation V2 provides a guided PMP-specific setup flow, helping you review deal terms and selected inventory before publishing. V1 users can also select Fixed Price directly during PMP deal creation.

**Planned release: 03 November 2026**

<Note>
  This release is planned. Availability depends on production deployment confirmation.
</Note>

## What is changing

- A five-step PMP V2 creation flow: Deal Information; Buyer, Seller, DSP & Commercial Terms; Inventory Selection; Creative Approval; and Review & Publish.
- PMP creation is submitted through the PDP API as an Endeavor deal, with or without a Media Spend Goal.
- You can optionally set a deal-level Floor Price/CPM. If left blank, each selected screen uses its inventory CPM as its floor.
- Creative Approval defaults to **Not Required** and can be enabled during setup.
- **Save as Draft** keeps the deal in Lynx without publishing to DSP targets.
- **Save and Publish** submits the deal.
- Deal details show deal status and permission-controlled **Pause** and **Resume** actions.
- V1 PMP creation includes a selectable **Fixed Price** auction type.

## How to use after release

1. Open **Deal Management** and start the V2 PMP creation flow.
2. Enter the deal identity, time zone, currency, flight dates, and DSP targets.
3. Select the buying party and enter seller contact details. Buyer contact and Media Spend Goal are optional.
4. Configure the supported auction terms. Optionally enter a deal-level Floor Price/CPM.
5. Select eligible inventory using search and filters as needed.
6. Choose whether Creative Approval is required.
7. Review all sections.
8. Select **Save as Draft** to keep the deal unpublished, or **Save and Publish** to submit it.
9. From deal details, use **Pause** or **Resume** when the action is available for your permission and the deal state.

For V1 PMP deals, select **Fixed Price** in **Auction Type** during creation.

## Good to know

- Leaving the deal-level Floor Price/CPM blank uses each selected screen's inventory CPM.
- Existing deals are not described as automatically migrated by this release.
- Production deployment and final supported auction behavior must be confirmed before this page is announced as released.
- Availability of Pause and Resume depends on your permission and the confirmed deal state.
