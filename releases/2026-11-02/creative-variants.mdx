## Creative Variants

**Planned release: 02 November 2026**

Preparing separate video files for different screen resolutions previously added manual work and made asset selection harder. Creative Variants lets you generate resolution variants from an eligible video in the Media Library, then choose the version that fits your inventory.

### What is changing

For eligible MP4 video creatives, use the **Generate** action to create standard resolution variants. Uploading a file and generating its variants are separate steps. The original file is preserved as the **Master**, alongside the generated variants:

| Version | Resolution | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Master | Original uploaded file | Source video asset |
| High | 1920 × 1080 | Full HD variant |
| Medium | 1280 × 720 | HD variant |
| Low | 854 × 480 | SD variant |

Each uploaded file and generated variant receives its own unique **Creative ID**. You can select and assign each variant independently to your media plans or filler content.

### How to use

1. Open **Libraries → Media Library** and upload your MP4 video creative.
2. Monitor upload progress in the **Upload Manager** at the bottom-right.
3. Once the file is Ready, click the **Generate** action on the master video row.
4. Track variant generation progress in the **Conversion Manager**.
5. When complete, expand the master creative row to view all variants.
6. Select and assign the appropriate resolution variant to your target inventory.

### Important behavior

- **Manual trigger:** Video variant generation is started manually by clicking Generate.
- **Master protection:** A master creative cannot be deleted while its variants exist. Delete variants first before removing the master asset.
- **Media types:** Variant generation applies to eligible MP4 videos. Images and raw files are not converted into video variants.
